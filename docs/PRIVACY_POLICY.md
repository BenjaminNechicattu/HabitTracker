# Habitty Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Habitty** (package name: `com.habitty.app`) handles your information.

## 1. Information we process

Habitty is designed to work primarily on your device.

The app may process the following information:

- Habit data you create (for example, habit names, categories, reminders, check-ins, and progress history)
- Profile information you set (for example, display name and avatar choice/photo)
- App settings (for example, theme, stats layout, reminder preferences)

This information is stored locally on your device using app storage.

## 2. Permissions

Habitty may request the following permissions:

- **Notifications**: to schedule and deliver habit reminder notifications
- **Camera / Photos**: only if you choose to take or select an avatar image

Permissions are optional except where required for that feature. You can manage permissions in your device settings.

## 3. Data sharing

Habitty does **not** sell your personal data.

Habitty does **not** send your habit/profile data to a dedicated Habitty backend server at this time.

Platform providers (such as Google Play) may process technical data needed to distribute and operate the app under their own privacy terms.

## 4. Data retention and deletion

Your app data stays on your device unless you remove it.

You can delete local app data from within the app (for example, using the in-app clear/reset option) or by uninstalling the app.

## 5. Children

Habitty is not intentionally directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a backend service.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to a local-first mobile app. However, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by changing the "Last updated" date above.
